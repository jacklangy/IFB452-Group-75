🛡️ Regulator Controls — Settlement

Confirm Delivery

Oracle has verified energy delivery. Confirm to release ETH to the producer.

Resolve Dispute — Proceed

Override dispute: energy was delivered. Release ETH to producer.

Resolve Dispute — Reject

Override dispute: energy was NOT delivered. Reject settlement (then use Trade Matching to cancel the match and refund consumer).

How Settlement Works

The oracle-verified payment flow from locked match to final ETH release

1️⃣ Match Locked

SC3 locks a bid to a listing. Consumer ETH is held in the TradeMatching contract, awaiting delivery confirmation.

2️⃣ Oracle Confirms

Smart-meter data is read off-chain by the regulator acting as oracle, verifying that the agreed kWh was delivered.

3️⃣ Regulator Calls SC4

The regulator calls confirmDelivery(matchId) on this Settlement contract, which updates the energy ledger.

4️⃣ ETH Released

SC4 calls SC3.confirmSettlement(), which transfers the locked ETH directly to the producer's wallet.

⚠️ Dispute Flow

Either party can raise a dispute before settlement. The regulator reviews and either proceeds (pays producer) or rejects (refunds consumer via SC3).

📊 Energy Ledger

Every confirmed settlement updates on-chain balances: kWh delivered/received and ETH earned/spent per address — fully auditable.
