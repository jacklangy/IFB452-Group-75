🛡️ Regulator Controls — Trade Matching

Lock Match (by Bid ID)

Normally called by the producer. Regulators can also lock a match.

Confirm Settlement (by Match ID)

Confirms energy delivery and releases ETH to the producer.

Flag Suspicious Match

Flags a Locked match for manual review. Pauses settlement.

Cancel Flagged Match

Cancels a Flagged match and refunds ETH to the consumer.
