How Listings Work

The lifecycle of every energy listing on the blockchain

1️⃣ Producer Submits

A verified producer posts kWh, asking price, time window and grid zone. The contract validates inputs against regulator price bounds.

2️⃣ Status: Open

The listing is written on-chain and marked Open. All members can browse and place bids.

3️⃣ Status: Matched

When a bid is accepted, the listing is locked as Matched and the consumer's ETH is held in escrow.

4️⃣ Status: Settled

Once smart-meter data confirms delivery on-chain, payment is released to the producer and the listing is marked Settled.

⏱️ Status: Expired

If no match is made within the time window, the listing is automatically marked Expired.

🛡️ Regulator Oversight

The AER can audit all listings, set price bounds, flag non-compliant entries, and manage producer registrations at any time.
