How Listings Work

The lifecycle of every energy listing on the blockchain

1️⃣ Producer Submits

A verified producer posts kWh available, asking price, time window and grid zone. SC2 validates all inputs against regulator price bounds.

2️⃣ Status: Open

The listing is written on-chain and marked Open. All platform participants can now browse and place bids through SC3.

3️⃣ Status: Matched

When a consumer's bid is accepted, SC3 locks the listing as Matched and freezes the consumer's ETH in escrow.

4️⃣ Status: Settled

Once oracle-verified smart-meter data confirms delivery, SC4 releases payment to the producer and marks the listing Settled.

⏱️ Status: Expired

If no match is made within the time window, the listing is automatically marked Expired and removed from the active market.

🛡️ Regulator Oversight

The AER can audit all listings, set price bounds, flag non-compliant entries, and approve or revoke producer registrations at any time.
