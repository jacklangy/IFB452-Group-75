How Listings Work

The lifecycle of every energy listing on the blockchain

1️⃣ Member Posts

A verified member calls listEnergy() with their kWh amount and price per kWh in Wei. The contract records the listing on-chain.

2️⃣ Status: Active

The listing is written on-chain with isActive = true. All members can browse it in the marketplace table.

3️⃣ Cancel Listing

The original seller can cancel their listing at any time by calling cancelListing(id), setting isActive = false.

🛡️ Regulator Oversight

Regulators can view all listings including inactive ones and have visibility over the full marketplace state.
